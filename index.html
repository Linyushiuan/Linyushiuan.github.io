About Me

I completed BCIT's Computer Systems Certificate in December 2025, studying web development, systems analysis, and Agile project management. I'm actively seeking an entry-level IT support role in Greater Vancouver, with particular interest in healthcare IT environments.

Before moving to Canada, I worked as a Medical Laboratory Technologist in Taiwan for over three years — performing blood collection, EKG administration, and laboratory testing across clinical chemistry, hematology, microbiology, and immunology. That role demanded the same qualities that make a strong IT professional: precision, strict protocol adherence, and calm problem-solving under pressure.

I currently serve as President of BCIT Women in Computing, organizing professional development events and connecting students with industry opportunities.
